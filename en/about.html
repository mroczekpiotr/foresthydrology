ABOUT

ForestHydrology.com is an independent knowledge platform focused on forest–water interactions and the application of GIS, remote sensing and hydrological modelling.

Purpose

The goal is to connect scientific concepts with reproducible spatial workflows, open data and practical examples.

What you will find

Articles, GIS tutorials, remote-sensing methods, hydrological modelling workflows, datasets and interactive WebGIS experiments.

Approach

Methods are presented with attention to assumptions, spatial scale, data quality, uncertainty and reproducibility.
