About ForestHydrology.com

ForestHydrology.com is an independent, multilingual science and education outlet dedicated to forest hydrology — the study of how forests shape the movement of water through nature.

Our goal is to combine sound scientific knowledge with accessible writing. We publish articles grounded in the scientific literature, explain the research methods used in forest hydrology, and describe why forests are a critical part of catchment water management. The site is developed in the open — its source code is public, and content is translated into four languages to reach a wider, international readership.

Rigor

Articles reference peer-reviewed scientific literature and clearly separate facts from interpretation.

Accessibility

We translate complex hydrological concepts into language non-specialists can follow.

Openness

The site is an open-source project, and content is published in four languages.
