Índices de água na deteção remota: NDWI, NDMI, MNDWI, AWEI e outros

Piotr Mroczek · 4 September 2026
#ForestHydrology#Hydrology

Spectral indices transform reflectance in selected bands into a value that highlights a particular surface property. In hydrology, the first distinction should be between indices for open water and indices describing vegetation water content.

Why can NDWI mean two different things?

McFeeters (1996) proposed a green–NIR NDWI for open-water mapping. Gao (1996) proposed an NIR–SWIR formulation for vegetation liquid water content; it is now commonly called NDMI.

IndexFormulaMain use
NDWI McFeeters(Green − NIR)/(Green + NIR)Open water
NDMI / NDWI Gao(NIR − SWIR)/(NIR + SWIR)Vegetation moisture
MNDWI(Green − SWIR)/(Green + SWIR)Open water; useful in built-up settings
AWEISeveral formulationsWater extraction and shadow suppression
WRI(Green + Red)/(NIR + SWIR)Water extraction
LSWI(NIR − SWIR)/(NIR + SWIR)Surface/vegetation water signal

There is no universal threshold

Thresholds depend on sensor, season, atmosphere, turbidity, shadows and land cover. An index is a proxy, not a direct hydrological measurement. Combining spectral indices with DEM, soils, meteorology and field data is usually more defensible.

Sentinel-2

Sentinel-2 provides green, NIR and SWIR bands at useful spatial resolutions. A reproducible workflow should state the exact bands and preprocessing method.
